From the publisher:

Get the best out of Power BI by building optimized data models for reporting and business needs

Highlights

  • Report quality is decided at the model, not the visual — Bakhshi's whole premise is that an optimized star-schema model upstream determines everything downstream in Power BI; the MDDE claim (get the model right and the rest follows) applied to the BI layer.
  • Transformation belongs in a defined layer — data preparation goes in Power Query or the source, analytical logic in DAX measures; assigning each kind of logic a designated home is layer discipline, the antidote to the untraceable everywhere-logic that makes BI solutions unmaintainable.
  • Star schema as the load-bearing pattern — dimensional modeling is not one option among many but the shape the VertiPaq engine and DAX are built around; a reminder that pattern conformance, not cleverness, is what scales.
  • Calculated column vs measure is an architectural decision — where a piece of logic lives changes storage, performance, and reuse; placement rules for logic are as much part of the model as the tables.
  • Row-level security as declarative model metadata — access rules expressed inside the model rather than per report is rules-as-data: one definition, enforced everywhere it's consumed.
  • Shared datasets and dataflows treat the model as a product — building the model once and letting many reports consume it is the reuse economics MDDE argues for at warehouse scale, played out inside the Microsoft stack.
