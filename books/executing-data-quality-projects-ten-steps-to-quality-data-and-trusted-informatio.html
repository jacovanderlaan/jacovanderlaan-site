Highlights (AI · MDDE/SBM lens)

AI-distilled highlights, not personal reading highlights — the ideas in this book that connect to Jaco's MDDE / Structure-Beats-Magic work, selected and paraphrased through that lens. Paraphrased (never verbatim); verbatim quotes await the book text.

  • Ten Steps: a quality methodology you can actually execute — McGilvray turns "improve our data" from aspiration into a numbered, repeatable process with entry and exit criteria; methodology as the antidote to heroic one-off cleanups, the DQ counterpart to model-driven repeatability.
  • The information life cycle (plan, obtain, store, maintain, apply, dispose) locates the defect — tracing where in the life cycle quality breaks tells you where the fix belongs; the same upstream-first instinct as fixing the model instead of patching every pipeline downstream.
  • Business impact techniques justify the rules budget — her toolkit for quantifying what poor data costs (from anecdotes to full cost analyses) is how a rules/quality layer wins funding against feature work; rules are the forgotten pillar partly because nobody priced their absence.
  • Data quality dimensions are chosen per assessment, not applied wholesale — the method selects only the dimensions that matter for the business issue at hand; scoped, purpose-driven checking rather than exhaustive checkbox governance.
  • Root cause, then prevention, then correction — the step ordering insists that cleansing without addressing cause is rework on a schedule; structure addresses the source, magic scrubs the symptom quarterly.
  • Sustainability is a step, not an afterthought — the method ends with embedding controls and monitoring so improvements persist; quality as an operating rhythm, exactly what automated checks-in-DuckDB style tooling operationalizes.

Structure beats magic.
