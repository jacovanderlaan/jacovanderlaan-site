Databricks Data Intelligence Platform - Sample

Nikhil Gupta, Jason Yip · 2024 · data architecture

Highlights

AI-assisted.

  • The lakehouse repackages warehouse discipline — Delta Lake's ACID tables, schema enforcement, and governance via Unity Catalog are the old structural virtues re-implemented on files; a platform conceding that schema-on-read magic needed structure back.
  • "Data intelligence" is platform + AI, with rules implied but underweighted — the pitch fuses data management and AI on one platform; through the missing-system lens the interesting question is where the business-rules layer lives, because the platform brochure rarely answers it.
  • Centralized catalog as the control plane — putting lineage, access, and metadata in one governed catalog is the platform-vendor version of the claim that metadata, not compute, is where control belongs.
  • ⚠️ _Sparse metadata — sample edition, verify against the book; highlights inferred from platform knowledge rather than the text._
