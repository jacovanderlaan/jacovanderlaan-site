Genie Meets the Cookbook

AI assistants as sous-chefs working inside a structured recipe DSL.

The framing of AI assistants as sous-chefs: they don't improvise SQL freehand, they work inside the structured Recipe Cells DSL, reading the concern of each cell and filling in the how. The cookbook (the patterns and rules) constrains the genie, so AI help stays deterministic and reviewable rather than a fresh guess each run.
