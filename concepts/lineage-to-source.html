Lineage & governance

Lineage to Source

Everything is logged and git-versioned, so you can see how the company brain develops over time and trace every fact back to the source it came from.

Knowledge you can&rsquo;t trace is knowledge you can&rsquo;t trust. Lineage-to-source is the property that every fact in the company brain carries its provenance: which document, which SQL, which interview it came from &mdash; and every change is logged and git-versioned, so you can see how the brain developed over time. Ask &ldquo;where did this definition come from, and who changed it, and when?&rdquo; and the answer is in the record, not lost. Two kinds of history matter here. Provenance points backward to the origin: this rule was extracted from that transformation, this definition came from that stakeholder interview. Version history shows evolution: because the metadata files live in git, you can watch the brain grow, diff two states, and roll back a mistake. Together they mean the brain is not a black box &mdash; it&rsquo;s an auditable, explainable body of knowledge. This is what makes the whole pipeline accountable. It pairs with model versioning (how the model itself changes) and underpins accountability: you can only stand behind an answer if you can show where it came from. Log everything, version everything, and the company brain stays trustworthy as it grows.
